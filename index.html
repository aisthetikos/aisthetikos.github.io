Farinelli Breathing Exercise

Learn breath control through the ancient technique

About This Exercise

The Farinelli breathing exercise is designed to teach breath control, not lung capacity. You'll take the same amount of breath throughout, but gradually extend the duration of each phase.

Key Principles:

  • Same breath volume throughout the entire exercise
  • Only the timing changes - inhale, hold, and exhale for the same count
  • No breaks between cycles - continuous flow
  • Control air with your body, not your throat

How to Use:

  1. Set your starting count (3-12) and maximum count
  2. Click "Start Exercise" and listen for the gentle chimes
  3. Follow the phases: Inhale → Hold → Exhale
  4. The count increases by 1 each cycle automatically
  5. Focus on maintaining the same breath volume throughout
